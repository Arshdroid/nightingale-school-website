About Our School

Nightingale International Secondary School is an educational institution located in Kupondole, Lalitpur, Nepal. Established in 1992, the school aims to provide quality education and help students develop knowledge, skills, discipline and confidence.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a supportive learning environment where students can develop their abilities, become responsible individuals and prepare for the future.

Our Vision

Our vision is to encourage students to become knowledgeable, confident and responsible members of society.

Facilities

  • Classrooms
  • Library
  • Science laboratories
  • Computer facilities
  • Sports and extracurricular activities
